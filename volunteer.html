Gear is only half the mission — it takes people to collect it, sort it, and get it into the right hands. Here’s how you can help.

Why volunteer

Join our team and make a difference

Are you passionate about fencing and want to help make the sport more accessible to everyone? We’re looking for enthusiastic volunteers to support our fencing gear donation project! Whether you can help with outreach, gear collection, sorting, or matching donated gear with people who’ve requested it, your time and energy will make a big impact.

  • Outreach to clubs, schools & fencers
  • Gear collection, sorting & inspection
  • Matching requests & coordinating pickup/delivery

Ready to get involved? Fill out our volunteer interest form and become part of a community working to grow fencing for all.
