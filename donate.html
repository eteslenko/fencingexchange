What we accept

Gear that's ready for a second life

We accept all types of fencing equipment as long as it’s in safe, usable condition — masks, jackets, gloves, shoes, blades, and more. Gently used gear that’s just sitting in a closet can be exactly what a beginner needs to get started.

  • Masks, jackets & plastrons
  • Gloves, shoes & socks
  • Blades, bags & other equipment
